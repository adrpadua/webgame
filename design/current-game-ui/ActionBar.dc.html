The Action Bar
A twelve-unit ladder: 2 | 4 | 4 | 2
The rails take two units each and the replaceable Slots share the eight between them. Undo and the advance control are the two most-pressed things in the interface, and the ladder exists to put them under the thumb. A grid rather than a flex row because the ladder is the contract: the Slots must not resize when a rail changes what it is. The Signature Slot never renders here — its face is the Hero Frame's control (D-065), so the bar carries exactly the Slots a hand card can reach.
Empty — nothing prepared
Loaded — a Top Card, no Charge
Charged — the stack building
Full — the ward ring closes, the gaps shut
Fired — spent this window, glass strike on the head
Out of window — Full's gold cannot claim “can fire”
A Slot's state lives in two channels at once. The leading-edge accent is the status channel every plate shares. The lock head is the glance-distance signal: its ward ring is broken while charging and closed at full stack with a second faint ring outside it — a ring closing is a shape change, so it reads in peripheral vision and without colour. The tumblers say the same thing a second way: separate raked pins with gaps while charging, and when the last one seats the gaps close and they read as one continuous bar.
