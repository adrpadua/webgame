The Hand
Three faces, one row — handFace.ts
What each Compact Card shows is the face the current gesture calls for. A card keeps one width — its share of a full Hand — whether five remain or one: cards that stretched to fill the row stopped reading as cards.
Card face — Loadout: what a Slot would get
Keyword face — Quick and Slow: what a Slot is hunting for
Stamina face — a move lined up: one card, one hex
The card face describes the card as a Top Card, which is exactly what Loadout is choosing. The keyword face drops the speed word and the Charge pips — both describe a Top Card, and a card being tucked adds one Charge whatever its own speed says — and promotes the Hero's own Keyword marks, gold on the ones a loaded Top Card would pay off for. The Stamina face makes the whole Hand a stack of interchangeable coins, because looking like anything else would suggest the choice of which one to spend matters more than it does.
