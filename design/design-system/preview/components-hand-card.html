Card face — Loadout
Keyword face — Quick and Slow
Stamina face — a move lined up
What a Compact Card shows is the face the current gesture calls for. The card face describes it as a Top Card, which is what Loadout is choosing. The keyword face drops the speed word and the Charge pips — both describe a Top Card, and a card being tucked adds one Charge whatever its own speed says — and promotes the Hero's marks, gold on the ones a loaded Top Card would pay off for. The Stamina face makes the Hand a stack of interchangeable coins, because looking like anything else would suggest the choice of which to spend matters more than it does.
A card keeps one width — its share of a full Hand — whether five remain or one. Cards that stretched to fill the row stopped reading as cards.
