State lives in two channels at once. The leading-edge accent is the status channel every plate shares. The lock head is the glance-distance signal: its ward ring is broken while charging and closed at full stack with a second faint ring outside it — a ring closing is a shape change, so it reads in peripheral vision and without colour. The tumblers say it a second way: separate raked pins with gaps while charging, and when the last one seats the gaps close and they read as one continuous bar.
Full and Fired share a complete Charge Stack and mean opposite things, so they cannot share a picture. Fired reopens the ring, dulls the head and draws one runeglass strike across it. An out-of-window Slot drops to the dim face before Full is consulted: Full's gold says “can fire”, which is the one claim it must not make.
