# Lore Dictionary Of The Warded Reaches

Status: active setting dictionary. Use this for consistent terms in story, card names, encounter flavor, art prompts, and onboarding copy. This document does not create gameplay rules.

## A

### Aether Relay

Type: Technology

A runeglass-and-signal-cloth system that carries warnings, command phrases, public counts, and battlefield reports across distance. Aether relays are physical towers, not invisible radio. They flash, hum, unfurl, and sometimes lie when damaged.

### Ashwake

Type: Historical Era / Phenomenon

The modern increase in raid-scale disasters, boss awakenings, and oathcraft failures beginning around 612 FB. Scholars disagree whether it is a natural cycle, sabotage, or a symptom of something beneath the world changing state.

## B

### Boss Instrument

Type: Technology / Threat Category

A self-sustaining catastrophe body built or altered to repeat a battle protocol. Boss instruments were banned by the Quiet Concord, but many were buried rather than destroyed.

### Blue Lantern Fleet

Type: Organization / Vehicle Network

Civilian evacuation barges protected by treaty. Their blue lanterns signal noncombatant passage. Attacking one is among the most serious crimes in the Warded Reaches.

## C

### Civic Count

Type: Law / Tradition

The verified count of living, missing, dead, and evacuated persons during a disaster. In Meridian law, many gates cannot close until the count is known or lawfully transferred to another watch.

### Concordance Of Gates

Type: Organization / Law Court

The treaty court that rules on evacuation rights, gate sabotage, passage tolls, disaster liability, and raid-company authorization.

## E

### Ember Coral

Type: Material

Organic red-orange mineral growth with heat veins. It appears in furnace regions and inside some heat-bodied bosses. It stores thermal intent, making it valuable and dangerous.

### Evacuation Oathcraft

Type: Natural Law / Civic Technology

The branch of oathcraft concerned with moving people safely under catastrophic conditions. It includes gates, signal roads, civic counts, ward cells, and sanctioned command phrases.

## F

### First Binding

Type: Historical Event

The shared signal-tower oath that marks year `0 FB`. It proved that public, witnessed protocols could produce stronger magic than isolated spellwork.

### Furnace Marches

Type: Geography

Black highlands and ember-coral vents east of the Meridian Rivers. Embermaw's current encounter style draws from this region.

## G

### Gate Engine

Type: Technology

A mechanism that moves, seals, redirects, or shapes passage. Gate engines can be city-scale infrastructure, battlefield rigs, or compact Hero equipment.

### Gate Rig

Type: Item / Hero Technology

A portable gate mechanism used by specialized defenders. Elian Voss's Gate Rig deploys runeglass panes and oathsteel ribs to create a defensive identity that reads like civic infrastructure under pressure.

### Gateblade Baton

Type: Item

A short command weapon used by gate watches to strike signal rhythms, anchor oathcraft panels, and deliver disciplined close-range attacks.

### Guarded Front

Type: Tactical Term

The dangerous space directly in front of a boss that a Tank deliberately claims. In fiction, this is not just "standing there"; it is taking responsibility for where danger lands.

## L

### Last Gate

Type: Title

Elian Voss's survivor-given title after Redwater Locks fell. It means the person who held passage open when the built gates failed.

### Living Gold

Type: Material

Muted brass-gold used in hinges, locks, oath mechanisms, and vow-authenticated devices. It remembers command phrases and authorized relationships.

## M

### Medicant Rig

Type: Technology

Aether ceramic braces, restorative glyphs, and stabilizing vessels used by healers and field medics. A future Healer should feel like a recovery protocol with a body, not a generic staff caster.

### Meridian Concord

Type: Organization

Treaty network of river cities and canal settlements. Its highest values are passage, public count, and disaster coordination.

## O

### Oath Lock

Type: Technology / Law

A living-gold lock that authenticates a role, vow, command phrase, or safe passage. Oath locks can reject illegitimate authority.

### Oathcraft

Type: Natural Law

Protocol magic that binds intent, geometry, material, and public witness into repeatable effects. Oathcraft is strongest when its vessel, vow, cost, and pattern all align.

### Oathsteel

Type: Material

Dark metal with luminous inlay seams and hard geometric edges. It is used for armor frames, weapons, shields, and role-defining equipment.

## P

### Pattern College

Type: Organization

Academy and research body that studies telegraphs, boss behavior, raid sequencing, and protocol design. It is both respected and blamed when predictions fail.

### Pattern Projector

Type: Technology

A device that paints legal danger zones, safe lanes, route diagrams, or ritual geometry. In UI flavor, telegraphs are projected encounter geometry.

### Protocol Magic

Type: Natural Law

Another name for oathcraft. It emphasizes that magic is not vague willpower; it is a repeatable procedure with cost, vessel, geometry, and evidence.

## Q

### Quiet Concord

Type: Treaty / Era Marker

The treaty that banned self-sustaining boss instruments and established cross-border disaster response obligations.

## R

### Raid Company

Type: Organization / Profession

A licensed team trained to answer raid-scale threats. Raid companies are public heroes, legal liabilities, tactical specialists, and political tools.

### Redwater Locks

Type: Settlement / Lost City

A fallen canal-fortress city of tide gates, canal locks, flood doors, ward gates, civic gates, and signal gates. Elian Voss's home.

### Runeglass

Type: Material

Translucent hard-light crystal panes with internal glyph grids. Used for barriers, lenses, spell foci, telegraphs, and safe-lane projection.

## S

### Salt Quiet

Type: Geography

Southern salt flats where banned boss instruments were buried after the Instrument Wars. Its surface is calm; its underground history is not.

### Signal Cloth

Type: Material / Communication

Saturated cloth panels with faction marks, route lines, or warning trims. It is used in clothing, banners, evacuation routes, and role identifiers.

## W

### Ward Gate

Type: Building / Technology

A gate that separates a district into an oathcraft cell. Ward gates prevent one disaster from consuming an entire settlement at once.

### Warded Reaches

Type: World / Region

The known setting: linked nations and city-states that survive catastrophic entities through oathcraft, public infrastructure, and professional raid companies.

### Witness Registry

Type: Organization

Independent recordkeepers who certify vows, deaths, civic counts, contracts, and rescue claims. They are beloved when they preserve the truth and hated when the truth is inconvenient.
