## Premise

The known world is called **the Warded Reaches**: a chain of city-states, fortress cultures, river empires, relay sanctuaries, and scarred frontier marches that survive raid-scale disasters through oathcraft. People do not assume walls will hold forever. They build protocols, gates, vows, engines, evacuation routes, and Heroes.

A raid boss is not a random monster wandering into town. A raid boss is a catastrophic system with behavior: a living furnace, an ancient instrument, a corrupted oath-engine, a divine body without restraint, or a biological disaster that can be studied, baited, resisted, and eventually answered.

Heroes are people whose equipment, training, and vows can interface with those threats. They are not simply adventurers. They are specialized raid instruments with names, factions, wounds, and public expectations.
