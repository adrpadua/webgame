# Design Decision Log

This log records current design-team decisions and recommendations. It does not replace ADRs; promote a decision to an ADR when it creates a long-lived architectural or product constraint.

| Date | ID | Status | Decision | Rationale | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026-08-13 | D-001 | Adopted | `EncounterEngine` is the sole rules authority for content behavior. | Content text cannot claim an effect the engine does not resolve. | Existing SDK, content, and parity probes stay green. |
| 2026-08-13 | D-002 | Adopted | The current Embermaw slice is an eight-Round solo tank teaching encounter, not a complete raid proof. | It correctly proves Action Bar, facing, telegraphs, hazards, and card-fueled movement; it does not prove party roles. | New-player walkthrough and nine-probe suite. |
| 2026-08-13 | D-003 | Proposed | No Boss counter tag may promise `Kill Adds` unless the active encounter deck contains an executable Minion answer. | A visible callout must name an available response. | `whelp_clear` probe and player test. |

Superseded by the user-approved default-deck migration recorded in [issue 04](../../.scratch/product-backlog/issues/04-promote-aegis-starter-deck-to-shield-wall-kit.md): the five-identity list is now the live/default deck as a product exception, without claiming the playtest evidence this decision originally required. | Structured eight-Round playtests remain owed as follow-up evidence for the now-live list. |
| 2026-08-13 | D-005 | Proposed | Treat current Scorched as route blocking in player-facing copy until forced movement exists. | Its entry damage is not reachable through the current voluntary movement path. | Hazard behavior probe and future forced-movement probe. |
| 2026-08-13 | D-006 | Proposed | Build Whelp intent before Phase II. | Adds need a visible deadline before Phase II asks players to combine spatial problems. | Seeded Minion behavior and UI-intent probes. |
| 2026-08-13 | D-007 | Adopted | Elian Voss is a Shield Wall Tank. | MMO players should immediately recognize a defender who holds the dangerous space and protects the party. | Future deck playtests must show mitigation and positioning are its dominant decisions. |
| 2026-08-13 | D-008 | Adopted | Armor is a pure damage shield for Elian Voss. | Armor remains an easy-to-read defense layer. It does not become a spendable conversion resource. | Tank-card probes must verify that Armor absorbs damage before Health loss. |
| 2026-08-13 | D-009 | Deferred | Interception is Elian Voss's default ally-protection tool. | It creates a clear emergency-save moment and rewards placement without requiring allies to remain adjacent, but ally targeting and redirection are not yet under shared contract. | A future engine and UI extension must prove selected-ally damage redirection before the card is added to the live deck. |
| 2026-08-13 | D-010 | Deferred | An Interception redirects one next damage event and then expires. | A single reliable save protects a party member without canceling a full sequence of raid pressure, but the timing and expiry behavior still need executable multi-Hero coverage. | An interception probe must cover consumed and unused expiry paths. |
| 2026-08-13 | D-011 | Deferred | Elian Voss protects by holding the Guarded Front, building Armor, and using short-response Interception. | Guarded Front and Armor are already core to the fantasy, but the full three-part package remains deferred until Interception is implemented and validated. | A character playtest must show each action has a distinct use. |
| 2026-08-13 | D-012 | Adopted | Elian Voss is Captain Elian Voss, The Last Gate. | A grounded, disciplined defender gives the Shield Wall a distinct identity without borrowing the Paladin's holy spectacle or a battle captain's support language. | Future card text, art, and voice lines follow the Hold, Brace, Cover, Clear, Advance language. |
| 2026-08-13 | D-013 | Adopted | Whelp Clear and Slow Top Card acceptance remain isolated replay contracts until their probes pass. | The first deck expansion must prove a targetable Minion answer and standard full-charge cleanup without altering the live deck. | Seeded `whelp_clear` and `slow_top_card_cleanup` scenarios plus scene parity. |
| 2026-08-16 | D-014 | Proposed | Kessa Varn, The First Step, is the Vanguard-pattern second tank: Momentum tempo, Boss facing manipulation, and thin standing mitigation as the stated price. Design in [kessa-varn-design.md](heroes/kessa-varn-design.md). | The Second Hero Of A Role rule requires a distinct strength-and-price pattern; Elian holds the Warden pattern, so the second tank claims initiation and tempo. | Momentum and facing-turn engine seams, focused probes named in the design doc, and three new-player sessions. No runnable resources before the seams exist. |
| 2026-08-17 | D-015 | Adopted | Riposte Ready is cashed by any Boss-damage card for `+1`; a legal Shield Slam takes the full `+2`. Cards without Boss damage never consume it. User-approved; options and rationale in [riposte-payoff-deepening.md](design-proposals/riposte-payoff-deepening.md). | Controlled-cohort evidence showed the earned window converting in only one of three seeds because Shield Slam (2 of 20 cards) was its only spender; an earned proc that silently evaporates is the worst payoff failure mode. The graded cash-out keeps Shield Slam the ceiling while making the floor never-zero and adding spend-order mastery. | Riposte probe off-payoff coverage, web engine tests, and a fresh evaluation cohort under the new rule — prior cohorts are historical evidence for the old rule only. |
| 2026-08-17 | D-016 | Adopted | The raid is a team game: a solo Hero defeating a Boss is a tuning defect signal, never a success criterion. Evaluation cohorts record any solo Boss kill as a red-flag finding. | User-stated design principle. The solo slice tests role execution to the Round-4 checkpoint; Boss defeat belongs to a Party. An economy or deck that solo-kills is over-rich or under-pressured by definition. | The deck-evaluation rubric's solo-slice boundary carries the rule; future Party-scale criteria supersede the solo framing, not the principle. |

## Next Decision

After the controlled deck playtest, decide whether the five-identity test deck creates sharper Slot Tension than the two-card baseline without obscuring first-time learning. This is a creative tradeoff requiring player evidence, not an engineering choice.
