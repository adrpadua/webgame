# Use a portrait-first direct-manipulation combat HUD

The playable combat HUD prioritizes a thumb-reachable Bottom Interaction Zone: four equal Compact Cards at the bottom, the persistent Action Bar immediately above them, and the hex board in the center. Cards are prepared or charged by drag, loaded slots activate by tap, and the hero moves by dragging a hand card to a legal adjacent hex; persistent buttons are limited to phase progression and recovery because direct manipulation keeps spatial tactical play legible on a phone.

**Annotated 2026-08-19 (ADR 0033, D-065):** the persistent-button clause stands as written. The Signature — an ability with no card to drag, because its Charge is earned rather than tucked — is fired from a control that is *not* persistent: it mounts only in the window the rules would accept the press and leaves when they would not, so the HUD's persistent buttons remain exactly phase progression and recovery. The Hero Frame that carries the Hero's readout is persistent chrome, but it is read rather than pressed, and it floats over the board's bottom edge without displacing it. An earlier draft of ADR 0033 recorded this clause as superseded; that supersession was withdrawn when the control became transient.
