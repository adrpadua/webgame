# Resolve Boss tracks when their window opens

`advancePhase` resolves a Boss track in the batch that OPENS its window: leaving the Loadout Step lands in Boss Instant with the Instant Row already resolved, and ending the Quick Window lands in Boss Incoming with the Incoming Row already resolved. The batches that leave a Boss window carry only the boundary itself (plus the telegraph refresh on the way into Quick). Every boss fact is stamped with the Boss's own phase, and — the point of the change — the authoritative phase while the staggered playout replays those beats IS the Boss's window.

The problem being solved is that the close-of-window batching made the rules and the presentation tell different stories. Pressing Next out of Boss Instant resolved the whole Instant Row and advanced into the Quick Window in one batch, so while the playout director replayed the beats one Continue at a time, the engine — and every surface reading it — already said the window was the player's: the Round track lit Quick during the Boss's turn, and player actions (charging, firing, moving) were rules-legal while the Boss was still visibly acting. A presentation-only held phase was tried first (PR #13): the playout carried the phase the batch resolved from and every phase-naming surface showed it until the replay settled. It fixed the display but kept two truths — the HUD said Boss Instant while the rules accepted Quick-window actions — and each new surface had to remember to read the presented phase. Moving the batch boundary makes the single authoritative phase honest, so the held-phase machinery is deleted rather than extended, and input during the Boss's telling is now genuinely illegal instead of merely misleading.

The global event order per Round is deliberately unchanged: advance, Instant Row, advance, telegraphs, expiries and cleanup, advance, telegraphs, Incoming Row, advance, end-of-Round bookkeeping, advance. Only the batch boundaries moved. Every player window therefore sees exactly the state it saw before, so committed Scenarios replay to identical states at every step a player action resolves and to identical final states and fingerprints. Encounter Records exported under the old batching group the same facts under different advance steps, which is the accepted cost of a rules-shape change in a design-iteration workbench.

Next completes the picture on the client: it stands in for the Continue bar between paced moments and is inert while any moment is still playing, in both pacing modes, so a resolved track waits in the Boss's window until the telling is done and Next moves on. This keeps ADR 0023's seam intact — the playout director stays presentation-only, and the Phase Reveal remains one more moment in its sequence.
