# Give the primary Hero a persistent Hero Frame

The primary Hero gets one piece of persistent chrome: the **Hero Frame**, floating over the board's bottom edge. It absorbs the hero branch of the Stat Panel whole — name, health with the Armor overlay, Counter chips, the deck gauge — and it is where the Signature renders (D-057): the earned Charges as resource pips titled by the authored `resource_title`, around one permanent central control that is also the resource meter. The control never mounts or unmounts; it sits visibly dark while empty, holds steel while a bank waits out a closed window, and ignites gold when the fixed Slot can fire right now — the ignition is the "it appears" moment, as an event rather than as chrome popping into existence. Firing is a tap on the ignited control, through the same store path the Action Bar's Slots use. The decision is D-058; this ADR records what it supersedes and the constraints that keep it honest.

## What this supersedes

**ADR 0006's persistent-button clause, and only that clause.** ADR 0006 limited persistent buttons to phase progression and recovery so direct manipulation would keep spatial play legible on a phone. The Signature broke that premise: it is an ability with no card to drag — its Charge is earned, not tucked — so direct manipulation has nothing to manipulate, and the alternative shipped first (a third Action Bar plate) cost the two real Slots their width on a 390-point ladder. The clause becomes: persistent controls are limited to phase progression, recovery, and the Hero Frame's Signature control. Everything else in ADR 0006 stands — the Bottom Interaction Zone's order (board, Action Bar, Hand), drag-to-prepare, drag-to-charge, tap-to-fire, drag-to-move.

**The Stat Panel's "temporal, not spatial" identity, deliberately and by half.** The interface direction chose one tap-summoned readout over persistent gauges, and the board direction deferred per-token state until the multi-Hero party model. That tension ("board-first argues for putting it back… resolved deliberately rather than drifted through") is resolved here, on the Hero's side only: *your own* state is chrome, because the Signature made some of it into controls and currency — a resource that fuels an identity ability cannot live on a surface that is sometimes closed, and the bank-versus-cash read (D-057 decision 13) needs the pips visible through the windows the Signature cannot act in. The *Enemy's* state keeps the tap-summoned panel unchanged: the difference between reading your own machine and inspecting the thing trying to kill you is now spatial, which is the sharper version of the distinction the panel was built for. Tapping the Hero's tile pulses the frame — the tap points at the chrome that answers it — and never opens a panel.

## The constraints that keep it honest

- **The board never resizes for it.** The frame floats in the board container's spare height — the canvas is width-bound at 390 points and now pins to the container's top, so the spare height pools under the last hex row, which is where the frame and the dock's prompts live. This is the overlay contract, kept.
- **The frame is the dock's floor, not a dock member.** It never comes or goes, so it has no rank to claim in a transience-ordered column. The notification dock stacks upward from the frame's top edge; the smoke suite holds every floating member off the frame the same way it holds them off the Action Bar.
- **Untargeted Signatures only.** The control fires with a tap and carries no targeting flow; the catalog refuses a `fixed` card whose `target_type` is not `none`, so the gap is a load error rather than a latent one.
- **The earn stays visible where it happens.** A granted Charge ignites its pip on the frame and floats Board Feedback at the Hero's hex in the same beat; a wasted earn — a qualifying block at the full bank — floats in a lost tone, because overcap is the banking decision's one price and a silent waste is unlearnable.
- **Party seed, solo implementation.** The frame's shape is the first party frame — compact enough that a second could sit beside it when the multi-Hero model lands — but only the primary Hero's is built, and the board direction's per-token-state question stays deferred to that same milestone.

## Accepted costs

An Encounter without a Signature (the teaching slice) still carries the frame, minus the control — persistent chrome the first-turn script does not yet teach. The Hero's health moved out of the tap-a-piece vocabulary, so the first tile a new player taps (their own) now answers with a pulse instead of a panel; the frame being permanently present is what makes that answer readable. And ~60 points of the board container's spare height are now spoken for on the smallest supported phone — the direction doc's fit table is stale on this and the Stat Panel rows and needs its next measurement pass.
