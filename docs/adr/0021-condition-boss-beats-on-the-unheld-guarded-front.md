# Condition Boss Beats on the unheld Guarded Front

Boss Beats gain an optional numeric field that increases their effect while no Hero holds the Guarded Front, evaluated through the `isGuardedFront` predicate the board module already exports. This is the counter-pressure mechanism against boss-range camping: the Encounter Workbench's policy search found the dominant Embermaw line on its first seed — park at hex distance 3, outside the range-2 Cinder Breath cone where only Raking Claw still lands, run two Steady Strike slot engines, and win in Round 6 with 10 Health to spare. That line is legal, because a 2026-08-16 grilling ratified `boss_damage` as range-free and directed that counter-pressure be authored encounter content rather than a card range rule. A tank fantasy whose best play is standing as far from the Boss as the board allows undercuts the Shield Wall identity and drains Slot Tension of its positional half.

The field goes on the existing Beat schema rather than arriving as a seventh Beat kind or a general condition system. A new Beat kind would conflate a condition with an action and would carry the whole mechanic on one bespoke Beat; a general `condition` field is the right answer for the second Boss and premature for the first, given that Beat vocabulary is deliberately Boss-specific and hardcoded until a second Boss exists. A numeric field is purely additive, tunes across several Beats instead of one, and needs no new predicate — `isGuardedFront` is already implemented and exported. We rejected Boss pursuit, which requires Boss movement the engine does not have, and rejected longer-range or board-wide Beats, which make distance worse without making the front attractive: camping would stop winning without holding ever starting to.

Acceptance is mechanical, not a judgement call. The scenario generator that found the dominant line is re-run, and the `far` position must stop beating `stay` on most seeds; design review is a second gate rather than the only one. Because that search must run against the finished fight, this lands after the Phase II trigger and Minion intent, and the three committed Scenarios regenerate once at the end rather than after each step.
