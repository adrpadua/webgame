# Use a persistent action bar with charge stacks as the player action model

Superseded by [0008-use-persistent-charge-stacks-and-full-charge-cleanup.md](0008-use-persistent-charge-stacks-and-full-charge-cleanup.md).

Player actions will be organized around a persistent `Action Bar` that starts with two `Slots`, with progression unlocking a third. Each slot keeps its `Top Card` across rounds until it is replaced or removed by an effect; firing a slot requires at least one card in its `Charge Stack` and consumes those charged cards, replacing a top card costs `1 Tempo`, charged slots are locked from replacement by default, most cards have printed `Quick` or `Slow` speed, charging a slot costs `1 Tempo` per card, and the main source of player pressure is `Slot Tension` rather than raw resource spending. Charged cards go to discard, which shuffles back into the deck when the deck runs out. We chose this over reset-each-round hands or fully disposable action rows because it better delivers the intended MMO action-bar feel, makes boss scripting readable against persistent player plans, and creates a durable tactical trade-off between charging, firing, and replacing abilities.
