# Use persistent Charge Stacks with Full-Charge Cleanup

A Slot enters play with a Top Card at `0 Charge`. Tucking any hand card beneath it adds one Charge, regardless of that card's speed, up to the Top Card's printed Charge Value. A charged Slot activates once in its matching player window; activation does not consume its Charge Stack. The Top Card's printed rules decide whether and how the Keywords in that stack modify its effect. When a Slot activates while its stack equals its Charge Value, discard the Top Card and its full Charge Stack at the end of that matching window. A full unactivated Slot is `Primed` and persists for later activation or an explicit special interaction. This supersedes ADR 0002 because a persistent charge investment, rather than a disposable activation fuel, best supports the intended MMO action-bar feel and future class-specific full-charge mechanics.
