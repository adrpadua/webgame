  • ~400 lines of engine.test.ts fire blocks map to one seam

Incremental: start with removeEntity — it deletes the tripled invariant on day one.

Problem. board.ts has genuinely deep functions (traversalRoute is 153 strong lines) but the module is shallow: the struct is the real interface, callers reach through it 107 times, and the missing removeEntity forces resolve.ts to hand-roll piece removal twice — which is why the Counter-host-liveness invariant exists in three places.

Solution. Grow the interface the struct's users actually need — removeEntity first, facing next, geometry-over-BoardState after — and shrink direct struct access commit by commit. No big bang.

  • locality: host-liveness invariant stated once
  • the two raw deletes become one interface call
  • facing rules concentrate with the movement kinds

Top recommendation

Deepen the Fired Card into one module

It concentrates the most friction per unit of work: the engine's #3 churn file hosts a 258-line case whose logic is duplicated across a seam that leaks a mutable bag, the Reader grammar has no owner, and the repo has already proven the exact move on legality (PR #147) — same switch shape, same evidence pattern, mutation anchors re-pointed the same way. It also clears the ground for the Round seam (second pick, and the one that makes your Loadout fold cheap), since both currently meet in resolve.ts.
