# 02 — Persistent modifiers (P1)

Status: open

Temporary/ongoing rule changes as a first-class concept — source, subject, lifetime, rule, value — kept deliberately distinct from Counters (markers interpreted by Readers). Prove with a small real subset: temporary range, movement, and damage modification; deterministic expiration; source-disappearance removal; explicit stacking order. Modifier families are a closed vocabulary; every consumer of a modified rule must be forced to decide (the no-silent-partial-support invariant).
